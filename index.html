Questions

Everything you need to know

What is narcissistic abuse recovery?+
Narcissistic abuse recovery is the process of healing from emotional and psychological harm caused by a relationship with a narcissist. It involves rebuilding self-worth, identifying manipulation patterns, setting boundaries, and reclaiming your identity. The right tools make the process faster and less lonely.
Are these journals for beginners?+
Every product is designed for survivors at any stage — whether you just left, you're in the middle of processing, or you've been healing for years and want to go deeper. The prompts meet you where you are.
What is the Red Flag Decoder?+
A free AI-powered tool. Describe a behavior or situation and it identifies narcissistic abuse patterns — helping you trust your instincts again. Use it right here on this page, no login required.
Are these digital downloads?+
Yes. All products are instant digital downloads — PDFs you can use on any device or print at home. You get access immediately after purchase through Payhip or Gumroad.
What is She Still Built?+
A sister brand for women entrepreneurs — digital tools, ebooks, and resources for women building income after surviving difficult seasons. Because healing and building happen at the same time.

Because these are instant digital downloads, we don't offer refunds. But if you have any access issues, reach out and we'll make it right.
